## Running dev notes

Brainstorming, architecture notes, open questions, and in-progress thinking go in [running-dev-notes/](running-dev-notes/) at the **repo root**.

- **Naming convention:** `YYYYMMDD-short-topic.md`. Date-prefixed filenames sort chronologically and make the evolution of thinking easy to trace.
- **Diagrams:** commit both source (`.mmd`, `.excalidraw`, etc.) *and* a rendered image (`.svg`, `.png`). Renders serve readers who don't want to run tooling; source is for whoever needs to update the diagram.
- **Before starting new work**, skim recent dev-notes to avoid re-treading ground.
